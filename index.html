Dear Nathan,

I can't believe it has been a year already! It hasn't felt that long since I saw you in a different light. As you can see, I compiled many different moments since we have started dating in November. I was never one for pictures, but what I have learned is that I can never help myself with you. The greatest pleasure in my life has been to have met you and learned every part that makes you a wonderful person.

I could have never imagined myself falling in love with someone, much less such a caring person that has seen all my flaws and embraced them. Coming into Davis, I was not looking for anyone long term due to always believing I could never fall in love, then I met you. Obviously, I didn't fall in love at that moment but I always saw you as someone who deserves the best in life because you brought such happiness to everyone. I saw such joy and decided to embrace it for myself; lucky for me, you've already fallen for me long ago. Today I celebrate us, but most importantly, the love we share and hope to pass onto future generations.

You have become an important part of me that I will never let go and wish to protect until my last breath. Thank you for all the slow mornings, the loud laughs, the milestones, the celebrations we have attended, but above all the quiet moments were I get to remember why choose to fall in love with you every single day. If you ever doubt it, just know the choice of loving you is so easy, it is as easy as every breath I take.

Here's to a hundred more years of love and joy with my sunshine and rainbows. I hope to love you in every lifetime.

Always yours,
Amber
